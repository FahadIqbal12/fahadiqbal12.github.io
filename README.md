#
### Me
I’m Fahad. I am a systems architect, researcher, and tech entrepreneur focused on building intelligent digital infrastructure. My approach to engineering combines applied research with rapid execution—designing robust backend systems, multi-agent frameworks.

### History
- I’ve been coding since I was 14, starting with hardware experiments, radar systems, and hackathons, which quickly became my way of learning how to build real-world tech.
- At 16, during the height of COVID-19, I digitized over 100 local shops by building lean ordering apps so they could survive lockdowns without high agency fees. Since then, I’ve stayed focused on building at the absolute edge of technology.
- Co-founded **Route3** in school, an in-game gaming wallet that attracted backing from San Francisco crypto investors, and won 2nd place at NIT Durgapur's national hackathon with **Medichain**.
- One of my side project **MomentumPRO**, a productivity platform, which attracted 60K+ users in just few week.
- Engineered **G1**, a multi-superapp autonomous agent platform (built to execute complex, multi-step actions across systems).
- Now, I’m putting my biggest bet into **MWorld**, a platform to run Autonomous Organizations.
